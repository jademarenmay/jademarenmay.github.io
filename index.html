Philosophy of practice

What guides my work with students

“

Students can't learn when they're hungry, and they don't stay where they don't belong.

My practice starts with basic needs. At the University of Kansas I kept the Campus Cupboard stocked and organized drives so food, clothing, and essentials were there when students needed them.

It continues with belonging. Whether I'm advising a new student at orientation or training tutors on LGBTQ inclusion, I want every student to see that the institution was built with them in mind.

And it rests on evidence. My research on LGBTQ mental health and my assessment of a food insecurity workshop showed me that care improves when we measure what students actually experience.
